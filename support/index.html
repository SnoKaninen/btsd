Get help with BTSD

Include your app version, iOS or iPadOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. You can find the app version in Settings. Please avoid sending passwords, financial records or customer details.

Your data and storage

BTSD works on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 18 or later. Processing runs on your device. For iCloud sync, use the same Apple ID on your devices and check that iCloud Drive is available. You can configure additional storage in the app.

Review recognised details

Always check amounts, dates and categories after scanning. Tax classifications and deduction estimates are suggestions from bundled rule packages and must be verified for your circumstances.

Privacy questions

The developer cannot see your private iCloud records or retrieve your receipts. Use the app’s export controls to keep copies, and review the privacy policy before deleting data.
