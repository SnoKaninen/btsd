What BTSD is – and is not

BTSD helps you organise receipts and invoices, create invoices and estimate which expenses may be tax deductible.

  • Not tax, legal or financial advice. All classifications, deduction estimates, VAT treatments, exchange-rate conversions and reports are suggestions generated automatically from rule packages and the data you enter.
  • Rule packages may be incomplete. Tax rules are compiled from public sources and have not been reviewed by a tax professional. National limits, allowances and special cases may be missing or out of date.
  • Text recognition can be wrong. Amounts, dates, VAT numbers and other values read from documents must be checked by you before you rely on them.
  • Your responsibility. You are responsible for your bookkeeping, invoices, tax returns, VAT filings and record-keeping obligations, and for checking everything you submit to authorities, customers or your accountant. Consult a tax advisor for your situation.
