Privacy policy

Your Privacy, Clearly Explained

BTSD keeps receipt scanning, text recognition and financial record processing on your device. Your records can sync through your own iCloud account and storage you choose.

Last updated: 1 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-01.1

Bill · Track · Spend · Deduct

Your receipts, invoices and expenses.
Under your control.

Summary

  • No collection of app records by the developer. BTSD does not send your receipts, invoices or financial records to a developer-operated server. There is no BTSD account, analytics, advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • On your device. Scans, text recognition, classification, tax estimates and reports are processed on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Your storage. Records are stored locally and, when available, synced through your private iCloud database. Document copies can be saved in your iCloud Drive and storage you configure, including folders, NAS and WebDAV servers. Without iCloud, BTSD works locally.

You control your data

You are responsible for the records you enter or import into BTSD, checking their accuracy, choosing where to store and share them, and keeping any copies you need. If you use BTSD for business, you are responsible for handling customer, supplier and other third-party information appropriately and meeting your record-keeping obligations.

The developer does not receive your app records and cannot access or manage your private iCloud account, devices or external storage on your behalf.

For privacy questions, contact Rasmus Sundsten at Rasmus@sundsten.fi.

Data the app stores on your device

  • Details you enter about yourself and your businesses: name, address, tax residencies, tax and VAT identification numbers, bank details, company details.
  • Documents you scan or import (images, PDFs, e-invoice files), the recognised text and the values extracted from them.
  • Expenses, invoices, customers, suppliers, payments, mileage and home-office records, exports and an audit log of changes.
  • Settings, including credentials for a NAS/WebDAV server, which are kept in the iOS Keychain.

This data is stored in the app's private storage and protected by iOS data protection. You can additionally lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode.

Outside services the app contacts

BTSD only connects to the internet for these purposes:

  • European Central Bank (ecb.europa.eu, data-api.ecb.europa.eu) – to download public euro reference exchange rates, including automatic refreshes when enabled. Requests may include a currency and date range, but do not include your documents, amounts, names or financial records. The server receives connection information such as your IP address and the app’s user-agent header.
  • EU VIES VAT number check (ec.europa.eu) – only when you tap "Check registration online (EU VIES)" for a VAT number. The VAT number you check is sent to the European Commission's VIES service, which is governed by the European Commission's own privacy statement.
  • iCloud (Apple) – when you are signed in to iCloud, your BTSD data (books, documents' details, invoices, payments, settings) is synchronised through your own private iCloud database (CloudKit), and document copies are saved in your own iCloud Drive. Apple operates these services under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive these records or have access to your private iCloud database. If iCloud is unavailable, BTSD can continue locally.
  • Your NAS or WebDAV server – if you set one up, document copies are sent to the address you entered, using the credentials you provided.
  • Links you open – for example your accountant's portal or the sources of tax rules, opened in your browser.

E-mails to your accountant are sent with your own Mail app; BTSD does not send anything by itself.

Permissions

  • Camera – to scan receipts and invoices.
  • Photos – to import images you select.
  • Face ID – to lock the app, if you enable it.
  • Local network – to reach your NAS on your home or office network.
  • Notifications – if permitted, reminders for due invoices, missing receipts and exports are scheduled on the device. Reminder text may include names, amounts or other record details; lock-screen visibility depends on your iOS notification settings. Apple may also deliver background signals used for iCloud sync.

BTSD does not use GPS or background location tracking. Mileage entries are entered manually. Text recognition and classification run on the device; documents are not uploaded to a cloud AI service.

Diagnostics

BTSD includes no third-party crash-reporting or analytics SDK. If you choose to share diagnostics with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide crash or diagnostic reports under Apple’s privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain on your devices, in your private iCloud database and in storage you chose until deleted. Deleting a document removes its local records and related invoices, requests removal of its copies from BTSD’s iCloud Drive folder, and syncs the record deletion to your other connected devices. Other devices remove their local copies when the deletion arrives. Issued invoice numbers and audit entries may remain as part of the accounting record.

Settings › Reset app removes BTSD records, local document files and the app’s iCloud Drive folder, and synchronises record deletions to other devices using the same Apple ID. NAS backups, files exported to other folders, copies shared with others and system backups are not automatically erased. Sync and file deletion depend on service availability and may take time. Uninstalling BTSD alone does not remove all cloud or external copies. Keep any records you must retain before deleting them.

Your rights

You can review and correct your records in BTSD, export them, manage storage destinations and use the deletion controls described above. The developer cannot retrieve or delete records in your private iCloud account, devices or external storage on your behalf. You can also contact your local data protection authority; in Finland this is the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

For questions about this policy, contact Rasmus@sundsten.fi.

Support correspondence

If you contact the developer, the developer receives the contact details and information you choose to include in your message and uses them to respond and investigate the issue. Avoid sending financial documents, passwords or customer information unless needed for your request. Support correspondence is separate from BTSD’s app records. You can contact the developer about access, correction or deletion of information you supplied directly.

App Store purchases

Apple handles any App Store purchase and payment processing under its own terms. BTSD does not receive or store payment-card details.
